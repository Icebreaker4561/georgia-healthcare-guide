## Where is Zugdidi, Georgia? {#where}

In the west of the country. Georgia's [national tourism site](https://georgia.travel/cities-towns/zugdidi) calls it "the administrative capital of the Samegrelo Region".[^1] According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zugdidi), the town lies 318 km west of Tbilisi and 30 km from the Black Sea coast.[^6]

**The wider region.** The GNTA [regional page](https://georgia.travel/regions/samegrelo-zemo-svaneti) names the town as one of the two centres of Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti.[^14]

**Altitude.** Wikipedia puts the town at 100–110 metres above sea level.[^6]

**Population.** About 40,100 people live in the town, according to Wikipedia, as of 1 January 2024.[^6] We found no official figure for the wider municipality.

**Travel times.** By road, it is 4:50 from Tbilisi.[^1] More [below](#getting-there).

## Dadiani Palace in Zugdidi: the museum and the garden {#dadiani-palace}

It is a Neo-Gothic palace in the centre of the town, and today it is a museum. The national tourism site describes the building as "Neo-Gothic" and says it "stands in the centre of the city".[^1]

### Dadiani Palace museum: what is inside {#palace-museum}

The personal belongings of the princely family. "Today it acts as a Palace-Museum where you can see the Dadianis' personal items," the national tourism site says.[^1]

**The building.** "Built in the 17th century, the palace underwent a comprehensive reconstruction under the guidance of the English architect Edwin Race in the 19th century," according to the same site.[^1] Wikipedia, a secondary source, dates the building to the 19th century.[^6] We follow the national tourism site.

**The region's history.** The same page calls it one of the first European-style palaces built in the historical territory of Odishi, "today known as Zugdidi".[^2]

**The collection.** The museum "contains more than 44,475 antique objects", the GNTA [page on the residence and the garden](https://georgia.travel/zugdidi-dadiani-residence-and-botanical-garden) reports.[^2] Among them, the page adds, are "one of Napoleon Bonaparte's private belongings and also one of his death masks".[^2]

**A state museum.** The regional page of the same administration puts it plainly: "The palace is now a state museum".[^14]

### Zugdidi Botanical Garden {#botanical-garden}

The garden lies right beside the palace and was set up in 1840, by the GNTA account.[^2] The national tourism site describes "the botanical garden, situated right next to the palace, which was created in imitation of the Bois De Boulogne Park in Paris".[^1]

**How big it is.** The figures differ. Unique plants grow "across all 67 hectares of the gardens", according to the national tourism site;[^2] Wikipedia gives 26 hectares.[^6]

### Is there an official timetable for opening hours and tickets? {#opening-hours}

No. We found no opening hours or ticket prices on an official site. The national tourism site gives no hours, prices or phone number for the museum, and we did not find a museum website of its own.

## Things to do in Zugdidi and nearby {#things-to-do}

The palace and its garden in the centre; nearby, a dam to the north and the Black Sea coast, according to the national tourism site.[^1]

### The Enguri dam {#enguri-dam}

North of the town stands a dam that has been declared a cultural monument. "To the north of the city, you will find a unique dam on the Enguri River," the national tourism site says.[^1]

**Why it matters.** "The Enguri Hydroelectric Power Plant has been declared a cultural monument," the site adds, "as it is the world's third-highest concrete arch dam".[^1]

**Tours.** "Tours of the dam are available, as well as boat tours out onto the lake," according to the same page.[^1] It gives no prices or timetable.

### Anaklia and the Black Sea {#anaklia}

The coast is close. "A thirty-minute drive from the city is Anaklia, a resort town on the Black Sea," the national tourism site says.[^1] For the beach and swimming, read our [guide to the resort](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/anaklia/#beach).

## Zugdidi weather and climate {#weather}

Humid subtropical. Wikipedia, a secondary source, classes the climate as Cfa under the Köppen system.[^6]

**The averages.** Over the year, Wikipedia gives an average high of 20.6~°C and an average low of 10.4~°C, for 1991–2020.[^6] We found no official climate norms for the town.

**Hot months.** For heat and storms across the country, see [Georgia in summer: heat, UV and storms](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/best-time-to-visit-georgia/#summer).

**Our tip.** If you are heading on into the mountains, check the forecast for the mountains, not for the lowland town you leave from.

## How to get to Zugdidi {#getting-there}

By rail or by road. Fast service 870/869 runs daily from Tbilisi,[^7] and by road the national tourism site gives 4:50 from Tbilisi, 1:40 from Kutaisi and 2:00 from Batumi.[^1]

### Tbilisi to Zugdidi train {#train}

One fast service runs every day. The [Georgian Railway general schedule](https://www.railway.ge/en/traffic-general-schedule/) lists "870/869 Tbilisi-Zugdidi" under "Fast Trains", with the note "Daily".[^7]

**Journey time.** The general timetable does not mark which times belong to which direction, so we give no journey time.

**Routes and tickets.** See [Routes](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/georgia-trains/#routes) and [How to buy tickets](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/georgia-trains/#tickets) in our guide to Georgian Railway.

### By road from Tbilisi, Kutaisi and Batumi {#by-road}

The national tourism site gives times, not kilometres: 4:50 from Tbilisi, 1:40 from Kutaisi and 2:00 from Batumi.[^1]

**Kutaisi airport.** The GNTA [airports page](https://georgia.travel/coming-to-georgia/public-airports-in-georgia) places the airport "in the west of Kutaisi, 14 kilometers away from the city".[^5] We found no official time for the airport-to-town leg. See [Arriving at Kutaisi airport](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/kutaisi/#airport).

**From Batumi.** See the travel section of our [Batumi guide](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/batumi/#getting-there).

**Bus and marshrutka.** We found no official timetable or fares for buses or minibuses on these routes. See [What official advice says about marshrutkas](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/marshrutka-georgia/#official-advice).

### Zugdidi to Mestia: the gateway to Svaneti {#to-mestia}

About three hours by road. The GNTA [page on the mountain town](https://georgia.travel/en/destinations/mestia) describes a "3-hour-long drive" from Zugdidi that "sees the environment change from subtropical to subalpine".[^3]

**The road.** Beyond the dam, "a road goes between the cliffs and the rushing Enguri River, leading you to Mestia and Ushguli", the site's page on this town reads.[^1] The official pages give no distance in kilometres, so we give none.

**Before you go.** Read [Getting there](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/svaneti/#getting-there) and [The Svan towers](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/svaneti/#mestia) in our mountain guide, and [Night driving, mountains and winter](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/driving-in-georgia/#night-winter).

**Our tip.** Leave in the morning to have daylight for the road. If you hire a driver, agree on the trip before you set off.

## Zugdidi and the Abkhazia line: what governments advise {#abkhazia-line}

Both governments quoted here advise against travel to the occupied region. Neither says anything about the town itself.[^8][^9]

<!-- схема: img/zugdidi-abkhazia-advice.webp | Infographic headed "Abkhazia: governments and the law", five cards: the UK government — against all travel to Abkhazia, all but essential travel next to the line; the Government of Canada — avoid the occupied regions and all travel within 5 km of the line, except Highway E60; Georgian law — foreigners may enter only from the Zugdidi municipality direction; Other directions — prohibited and punishable under the Criminal Code of Georgia (Article 4); Zugdidi — neither government names the town itself; line "Neither government names Zugdidi"; source line: GOV.UK, travel.gc.ca, matsne.gov.ge, checked 22 Nov 2026 | Entry to Abkhazia from any direction other than Zugdidi municipality is prohibited and punishable under the Criminal Code of Georgia, says the Law on Occupied Territories -->

**The UK government.** Its [travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/georgia/warnings-and-insurance) says: "For security reasons, FCDO advises against all travel to the Russian occupied regions", and it names South Ossetia and Abkhazia.[^8] The UK government (FCDO) "also advises against all but essential travel to areas immediately next to the administrative boundary lines".[^8]

**The Government of Canada.** Its [travel advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/georgia) says to avoid all travel to the occupied regions of Abkhazia and South Ossetia and to areas "within 5 km of the administrative boundary line", "excluding Highway E60".[^9] The reasons it gives: "crime, military activity, the risk of detention and the presence of landmines".[^9] It also warns that the checkpoints "are subject to unexpected closures", and that landmines "may pose a risk where military operations have occurred".[^9]

**What the advice does not say.** Neither government names Zugdidi or says whether the town lies inside these zones. We found no official source on whether the crossing points with Abkhazia work today.

### Can you go to Abkhazia from Zugdidi? {#entering-abkhazia}

Under Georgian law, foreigners may enter the occupied region only from the direction of Zugdidi municipality; every other direction is prohibited.[^10] Both governments above advise against going at all.

**The law.** We quote Article 4 of the [Law of Georgia on Occupied Territories](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/19132) (English translation on Matsne; the latest consolidated Georgian text, of 26 June 2025, says the same).[^10] It lets foreign citizens in "only through the below route". For this region, the route is "from Zugdidi municipality direction".[^10]

**Other directions.** Entry from any other direction "shall be prohibited and be punishable under the Criminal Code of Georgia", says the same article.[^10] "In a particular case", it adds, a person may be granted "special permission to enter the occupied territories from the prohibited directions".[^10]

**Unofficial crossings.** The Government of Canada warns that entering Georgia from these regions at an unofficial crossing can bring "heavy fines and jail sentences".[^9]

**No help on the spot.** The UK government says it "cannot provide face-to-face support to British nationals" in the occupied regions.[^8]

For the rest of the country, see [Where not to go: Abkhazia and South Ossetia](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/is-georgia-safe/#where-not). Near the coast, see [The Abkhazia line near Anaklia](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/anaklia/#abkhazia-line).

**Our tip.** Do not try to cross the administrative boundary line. Before a trip towards the line, read the current advice of your government.

## Hospitals and medical help in Zugdidi {#health}

In an emergency, dial 112.[^12] On the national tourism site's list, the town has Zugdidi Referral Hospital.[^4]

### Calling 112 in Zugdidi {#call-112}

One number works around the clock, and its call-takers can handle calls in English. Georgia's [emergency service](https://112.gov.ge/?p=1715) takes calls 24/7 throughout the country; its "call-takers can handle calls in Georgian, English, Azerbaijani, Armenian, Turkish and Russian".[^12]

The UK government's [health advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/georgia/health) puts it simply: "Dial 112 and ask for an ambulance".[^11] Step by step: [How to call an ambulance](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/ambulance-georgia/#how-to-call).

**Our tip.** Say the name of the town first, then the street or a landmark, such as the palace.

### Where is the hospital in Zugdidi? {#hospital}

On K. Gamsakhurdia Street. The GNTA [medical information page](https://georgia.travel/coming-to-georgia/medical-information) lists the Referral Hospital at K. Gamsakhurdia St. №206.[^4]

**What we could not confirm.** We found no official word on whether it works around the clock or how it receives foreign patients. In an emergency, call the ambulance first.

**Outside the capital.** The UK government warns: "Outside Tbilisi, medical facilities are limited".[^11] The Government of Canada adds: "In rural areas, there are shortages of medical supplies and personnel".[^9]

**Clinics.** The [catalogue of clinics in Zugdidi on 100doc.ge](https://100doc.ge/en/zugdidi/) listed 39 clinics on 22 November 2026.

### Dogs and rabies {#dogs}

Keep away from dogs. The UK government says rabies "treatment is available in Tbilisi and major towns".[^11]

The Government of Canada warns: "Rabies is present in this destination and is carried by dogs and some wildlife, including bats".[^9] If an animal bites or scratches you, read [What to do after a dog bite](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/rabies-georgia/#first-aid).

**Your cover.** Visitors need health insurance of no less than 30,000 GEL (Georgian lari), says the UK government in its [entry advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/georgia/entry-requirements).[^13] More in [Insurance to Enter Georgia (2026 Rule)](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/#entry-insurance).

**Our tip.**

- Bring enough of your regular medicines for the whole trip.
- Before the mountains, save the emergency number and the K. Gamsakhurdia Street address in your phone.
- In the heat, drink water and keep to the shade. See [Tap water and bottled water](https://icebreaker4561.github.io/georgia-healthcare-guide/georgian-food/#water).

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Where is Zugdidi?

In western Georgia. It is the administrative capital of the Samegrelo Region.

### What is Zugdidi known for?

For the Neo-Gothic palace of the Dadiani family, now a Palace-Museum, and for the botanical garden beside it.

### How do I get from Tbilisi to Zugdidi?

By the daily fast train 870/869, or by road in 4:50. We found no official bus timetable.

### How do I get from Zugdidi to Mestia?

By road, in about three hours. We found no official marshrutka timetable or fares.

### How do I travel between Kutaisi and Zugdidi?

By road it takes 1:40. Kutaisi airport lies 14 km from Kutaisi.

### What is the weather like in Zugdidi?

Humid subtropical (Cfa in the Köppen classification); the averages are in the weather section above.

### Can I visit Abkhazia from Zugdidi?

The UK government and the Government of Canada advise against it. Under Georgian law, foreigners may enter only from this municipality's direction; any other route is punishable under the Criminal Code of Georgia.

### Where is the hospital in Zugdidi?

Zugdidi Referral Hospital is at K. Gamsakhurdia St. №206.

### What number do I call in an emergency?

Dial 112. It takes calls 24/7 across Georgia, and its call-takers can handle calls in English.
