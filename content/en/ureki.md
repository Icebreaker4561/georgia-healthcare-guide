> - A small Black Sea resort in Guria with a 1.5 km coastline of magnetic sand.[^1][^2]
> - Official pages make medical claims for the dark grains; the authors of a 2023 study left the effect on health for a later stage of their research.[^1][^3]
> - Summer runs from June to the middle of September.[^1] In 2025 the Ministry of Internal Affairs said its rescue teams would fully cover the coast of three regions, this one included.[^4]
> - The sand "retains heat for a long time", the tourism administration writes.[^1] Our tip: wear beach shoes at midday.
> - Emergency: dial 112, 24/7, with English-speaking operators.[^5]

> [!stats]

> - From Batumi: 55 min by road

> - Summer: June – mid-September
> - Sunshine: 1,800–1,820 hours (average)
> - Emergency number: 112

## Where is Ureki, Georgia? {#where}

Ureki lies in the Guria region of Georgia, "in the south-western part of the Kolkheti lowland", according to the regional administration.[^2] The resort is bounded by the sea to the west, by the mountains to the east, and by the Supsa and Natane rivers to the north and south.[^2]

<!-- схема: img/ureki-beach.webp | Illustration: dark grey and black sand on the Black Sea, turquoise water, mountains in the distance and simple figures at the water's edge | The regional administration dates the history of this place back more than 100 years -->

It "has officially existed as a resort since 1971", the same administration states.[^2] Wikipedia places it in Ozurgeti Municipality.[^6]

Its neighbour along the shore is Grigoleti. The Georgian National Tourism Administration describes the two together: their shore is five kilometres long.[^7] The neighbour belongs to Lanchkhuti Municipality, and the regional administration says its shore contains large amounts of magnetic iron particles.[^8] The same regional page also mentions Shekvetili.[^2]

## Is Ureki sand really magnetic? What official sources say {#magnetic-sand}

Yes, according to both official sources we checked. The regional administration describes the fine "velvety" grains as having "magnetic properties".[^2]

The tourism administration goes further and gives the mineral make-up: "The main minerals of Ureki sand are magnetite (5%), titanomagnetite (4.5%), monazite (4.9%), zircon (4.2%) and others."[^1]

What is magnetite? A 2023 study describes it as "an iron-bearing mineral with magnetic properties".[^3] On another page, the tourism administration calls the shore of Ureki and Grigoleti "gray and black sand containing magnetite".[^7]

Neither administration gives the share of iron or titanium as elements, or says how strongly the grains react to a magnet. We do not guess at either.

### Does the sand heal? What is claimed and what is proven {#health-claims}

Official pages say it does; the one scientific study we found, from 2023, leaves that effect for a later stage. Here is who says what.

**The tourism administration.** Its page says the grains create "a natural magnetic field of low intensity", with a healing effect "especially for musculoskeletal and cardiovascular system conditions".[^1]

**The regional administration.** It states that the natural conditions here can be used to treat diseases of the cardiovascular system, of the peripheral organs and of the musculoskeletal organs, in adults and children.[^2] The page gives no clinical data and cites no study.

**The 2023 study.** Its authors, publishing in the journal JECM, ran X-ray fluorescence and X-ray phase analyses of samples from Ureki and Chakvi.[^3] They write that the rare make-up of the local samples is likely to shape their effect on health, which they plan to study at the next stage.[^3] The 2023 paper gives no results for that stage.

### Sand baths: who should ask a doctor first {#sand-baths}

We found no official list of people who should avoid lying or burying themselves in warm sand. Nor did any official or scientific source we checked say whether the grains are toxic or safe.

**Our tip.** This is our advice, not an official rule:

- If you have a long-term illness or take medicine every day, ask your doctor first.
- Do not bury a small child in the middle of the day.
- If you feel unwell, move into the shade at once; if it does not pass, call the [emergency number](#call-112).

Another Georgian spa town with similar claims, Tskaltubo, has its own section: [Who should not take radon baths](https://guide.100doc.ge/tskaltubo/#who-should-not).

## Ureki beach: 1.5 km of grey and black sand {#beach}

The resort's own coastline is 1.5 km long, by the tourism administration's figure.[^1] Counted together with Grigoleti, the dark shore runs for five kilometres, the same administration states.[^7]

<!-- схема: img/ureki-shore.webp | Illustration: a long curve of dark sand along the Black Sea, with surf breaking on the shore and pale low land behind | The 1.5 km figure excludes Grigoleti; the five kilometres include it -->

The colour is official too: the administration calls it "gray and black".[^7] Not all of this coast looks like that. For a shore of pebbles, see [Batumi beach and the sea](https://guide.100doc.ge/batumi/#beach); for a mix of pebbles and fine grains, see [Anaklia beach: pebble and fine sand](https://guide.100doc.ge/anaklia/#beach).

No official page we checked describes its facilities or how the water deepens, so we leave both out.

## Can you swim at Ureki? Season, rescuers and children {#swimming}

Yes. It is a summer destination, and the tourism administration gives its summer as June to the middle of September.[^1]

<!-- схема: img/ureki-swimming.webp | Infographic headed "Swimming at Ureki", four cards: Season — June to mid-September (tourism administration); Rescuers — Guria coast to be fully covered (Ministry of Internal Affairs, 2025); Flags, currents, depths — no official information found, our tip: swim where rescuers are on duty; Trouble in the water — 112, 24/7 across the country | The 2025 statement names three regions, not individual places -->

What is known about safety in the water comes from one statement. In June 2025, at the start of the tourist period, the Minister of Internal Affairs said: "Rescuers in Adjara, Guria and Samegrelo will fully cover the area of the Black Sea coast".[^4] The statement does not name this place.

We found no official information on flags, currents or depths here, and no 2026 lifeguard schedule for it. Governments are silent on this too: neither the UK government (FCDO) nor the Government of Canada mentions swimming at the coast in its travel advice for the country.[^9][^10]

**Our tip.**

- Go into the water only where you can see a rescue team on duty, even if the water looks calm.
- Stay out after a storm, and after drinking alcohol.
- Keep children within arm's reach.
- If someone gets into trouble in the water, [call the emergency number](#call-112).

More about swimming on this coast: [Can you swim in the sea at Anaklia?](https://guide.100doc.ge/anaklia/#swimming)

### Ureki weather and the summer season {#weather}

The regional administration calls the climate here "humid, subtropical" and gives an average of 1,800–1,820 hours of sunshine, without naming the period.[^2] Summer dates are in the [section above](#swimming).

Neither the tourism nor the regional administration gives water or summer air temperatures, so we give none. For the warm months across the country, see [Georgia in summer: heat, UV and storms](https://guide.100doc.ge/best-time-to-visit-georgia/#summer). Check the forecast on the day you travel.

## Hot dark sand and the sun {#sun-sand}

The tourism administration writes that the sand "retains heat for a long time", listing this among its medical qualities.[^1] **Our tip:** do not walk barefoot on it in the middle of the day.

The sun is the other risk. The NHS warns that water reflects ultraviolet (UV) rays, increasing your exposure.[^11] Water also washes sunscreen off, and its cooling effect "can make you think you're not getting burned"; the NHS advises reapplying every 2 hours.[^11] It adds that sunlight reflected from sand and water is particularly dangerous for the eyes.[^11]

<!-- схема: img/ureki-hot-sand.webp | Infographic headed "Hot sand and sun", four cards: Dark sand — retains heat for a long time (tourism administration); Reflection — sunlight reflected from sand and water is particularly dangerous for the eyes (NHS); Sunscreen — reapply every 2 hours (NHS); Heatstroke — a medical emergency: call 112 (NHS, 112.gov.ge); at the bottom, our tip: beach shoes and shade at midday | Reflected light is particularly dangerous for the eyes, the NHS warns -->

Heatstroke is "a medical emergency", say both the NHS and WHO.[^12][^13] WHO advises drinking water regularly, "1 cup of water per hour and at least 2–3 litres per day"; that is its advice for hot weather in general.[^13] The NHS adds light-coloured, loose clothing and avoiding the sun between 11am and 3pm; it is a UK service, so these hours are not set for Georgia.[^12] Here, for suspected heatstroke, call 112.

Little ones need extra care. WHO advises wide-brimmed hats, sunglasses and sunscreen to protect children from the sun's rays.[^13]

**Our tip.** Wear beach shoes at midday. Bring your own shade, such as an umbrella. Keep the youngest and oldest in your group out of the sun in the middle of the day. More in [Staying healthy in the summer heat](https://guide.100doc.ge/batumi/#heat).

## How to get to Ureki from Tbilisi, Batumi and Kutaisi {#getting-there}

By road, the tourism administration gives 4 h 30 min from Tbilisi to Ureki, 55 min from Batumi and 1 h 40 min from Kutaisi.[^1]

**By marshrutka or bus.** No official timetable or fares turned up for shared vans or buses to the coast. See [What official advice says](https://guide.100doc.ge/marshrutka-georgia/#official-advice).

**By car.** Before you drive, read [Georgia roads: what to expect](https://guide.100doc.ge/driving-in-georgia/#roads).

**From the airport.** If you fly into western Georgia, see [Arriving at Kutaisi airport](https://guide.100doc.ge/kutaisi/#airport).

**Our tip.** Plan the drive back for daylight, and carry water on summer days.

### Is there a train to Ureki? {#train}

Services run to Ozurgeti, but the timetable does not show where they stop on the way. The general schedule of Georgian Railway lists only the end points of each route: the fast 854/853 Tbilisi–Ozurgeti every second day and the electric 613/614 Batumi–Ozurgeti daily.[^14]

**Our tip.** Before you travel, check on railway.ge or at the station where your service stops. Routes are in [Train routes in Georgia](https://guide.100doc.ge/georgia-trains/#routes).

## Medical help near Ureki: 112 and doctors {#medical-help}

In an emergency, dial 112 and ask for an ambulance, as the UK government's health advice puts it.[^15] The same advice warns that outside the capital, medical facilities are limited.[^15] No official list of hospitals for this stretch of coast or the towns around it turned up in our checks.

### Calling 112 at Ureki {#call-112}

One number covers every emergency, and you can speak English. The service states that it takes calls 24/7 across the whole country, in Georgian, English, Azerbaijani, Armenian, Turkish and Russian.[^5]

**Our tip.** Start with "Ureki, Guria". Then say exactly where you are: the shore and the nearest entrance or café, the road, or the train. More in [What to tell the operator](https://guide.100doc.ge/ambulance-georgia/#what-to-say).

### Where to find a doctor {#doctor}

We found no official list of medical practices here, and 100doc.ge has no page for this resort or for Ozurgeti. The [catalogue of clinics in Kobuleti on 100doc.ge](https://100doc.ge/en/kobuleti/) covers a different town; it listed 8 clinics on 21 November 2026.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### Can you swim in the Black Sea at Ureki?

Yes, in summer, from June to the middle of September.[^1] The Ministry of Internal Affairs said its rescue teams would cover this coast in full but named no beaches, so go in only where a rescue team is on duty.[^4]

### Is Ureki sand really magnetic?

The regional administration says it has magnetic properties, and the tourism administration lists its main minerals with percentages.[^2][^1]

### Is magnetic sand safe?

No official or scientific source we checked answers this. Our tip: if you have a long-term illness, check with your physician first.

### Do the grains heal anything?

Official pages say so; the authors of a 2023 study planned to research the effect on health at a later stage.[^1][^3]

### When is the season in Ureki?

From June to the middle of September, the tourism administration says.[^1]

### How far is Ureki from Tbilisi and Batumi?

About 4 h 30 min and 55 min by road. The tourism administration gives no distance in kilometres.[^1]

### Is there a train to Ureki?

The schedule lists only the end points of routes, so check on railway.ge or at the station whether a service stops there.[^14]

### Are there lifeguards at Ureki beach?

The ministry said in 2025 that the Guria coast would be fully covered; no 2026 schedule for this beach turned up in our checks.[^4]
