## When is Easter in Georgia? Dates for 2026–2028 {#when}

In 2027 the feast falls on Sunday 2 May,[^2] according to the Orthodox calendar.[^6] Orthodox Easter 2027 is five weeks after the Western date, 28 March.[^3]

The date moves but always falls in April or May.[^4] For April and May weather, see [Spring and autumn: Georgia in May and October](https://guide.100doc.ge/best-time-to-visit-georgia/#spring-autumn).

| Year | Orthodox | Western | Holidays (our count) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 12 April[^2][^9] | 5 April[^10] | 10–13 April |
| 2027 | 2 May[^2] | 28 March[^3] | 30 April – 3 May |
| 2028 | 16 April[^2] | 16 April[^11] | 14–17 April |

Our count from the law; the government may add extra days by ordinance.[^1] 9 April 2026 was also a day off.[^1]

### Why is Georgian Easter later than Western Easter? {#why-later}

The Orthodox calendar sets the Georgian date;[^6] the Western one is set differently.[^3] The gap: five weeks in 2027, none in 2028, one week in 2026.[^2][^10][^11]

## Public holidays at Easter in Georgia: what closes {#public-holidays}

Four Easter days are public holidays in Georgia: Good Friday, Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday and Easter Monday, which Article 30 of the Labour Code calls All Souls' Day.[^1] The same article lists Christmas on 7 January and Mariamoba on 28 August.[^1]

Government offices and many private businesses do not work; restaurants and cafés stay open.[^4]

**Our tip.** Get lari in cash before the long weekend; see [Cash or card in Georgia?](https://guide.100doc.ge/money-in-georgia/#cash-or-card).

We found no 2027 timetables. In 2026 the Tbilisi Metro was set to run free at Easter, from 23:00 to 04:00, and special buses to the cemeteries from 08:00 to 20:00.[^12] Extra trains were scheduled between the capital and Batumi and Poti for 8–14 April 2026.[^13] See [How to buy Georgian Railway tickets](https://guide.100doc.ge/georgia-trains/#tickets) and [Tbilisi metro hours and intervals](https://guide.100doc.ge/tbilisi-metro/#hours).

## Georgian Easter traditions: red eggs, paska and the night in church {#traditions}

Easter is the main religious feast for Orthodox Christians.[^6] It follows the longest fast, 40 days.[^5]

<!-- схема: img/easter-in-georgia-red-eggs.webp | Illustration: a clay bowl of red-dyed eggs beside onion skins, madder roots and a plate of sprouted wheat | The red egg is a symbol of the circle of life and rebirth -->

On Palm Sunday believers sprout wheat on plates.[^6] Eggs are dyed red on Good Friday, traditionally with rose madder roots and onion skins.[^5] Most believers spend the night before the feast in church.[^5] People also hold egg-cracking contests.[^5]

Before a service, read [Visiting churches](https://guide.100doc.ge/mtskheta/#church-etiquette); the UK government advises cultural sensitivity when photographing churches.[^14]

Chona, or Alilo, is a village custom: men sing from house to house, and the hostess lowers a basket of sweets, fruit and money from a balcony.[^6]

<!-- схема: img/easter-in-georgia-chona.webp | Illustration: a woman lowering a basket of fruit and grapes on a rope from a wooden balcony to villagers below | This custom is still kept in almost all parts of the country -->

### Easter Monday: a day for family graves {#easter-monday}

On Easter Monday people across the country visit their relatives' graves, pray for the dead and roll an egg over them.[^6]

### What do people say at Easter? {#greeting}

People greet each other with "Christ is risen!" and answer "Truly he is risen!"[^5]

## Easter food in Georgia: paska, lamb and the supra {#easter-table}

The raisin cake is the centre of the festive table and symbolises Golgotha.[^5][^6]

Lamb is eaten in the east, a suckling piglet in the capital and the west, with khachapuri, chakapuli, fish, greens and wine.[^5]

The Government of Canada advises: "Boil it, cook it, peel it, or leave it."[^15] See [Food safety rules for travellers](https://guide.100doc.ge/georgian-food/#safe-eating).

## Staying safe over the Easter weekend {#health}

Dial 112 for an ambulance; outside Tbilisi, medical facilities are limited.[^7] Water quality varies; bottled water is widely sold.[^7]

Crime levels are low, but watch for pickpockets in crowded places.[^14] Amid ongoing protests, the UK government advises avoiding large gatherings and demonstrations;[^14] the Government of Canada notes they are frequent, especially in central Tbilisi.[^15]

See [How to call an ambulance in Georgia: dial 112](https://guide.100doc.ge/ambulance-georgia/#how-to-call) and [Is it safe to travel to Georgia right now? The protests](https://guide.100doc.ge/is-georgia-safe/#right-now). For a non-urgent problem, the [catalogue of doctors and clinics in Tbilisi on 100doc.ge](https://100doc.ge/en/tbilisi/) is one place to look.

### Wine at the supra and driving {#alcohol-driving}

If you drive, do not drink: more than 0.3 ‰ of alcohol in a driver's blood counts as drunk driving.[^8] Up to 0.7 ‰ means a six-month licence suspension, more means a year.[^8]

The CDC advises limiting alcohol.[^16] The Government of Canada describes a "zero-tolerance policy" on drink-driving,[^15] and the UK government advises avoiding alcohol if driving.[^14] See [Drink driving](https://guide.100doc.ge/driving-in-georgia/#alcohol).

**Our tip.** Pick a sober driver before the meal.

**Before Friday 30 April:** get cash, buy train tickets early, save the emergency number.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq}

### When is Easter in Georgia in 2027?

Sunday 2 May, according to the Orthodox calendar.[^2][^6]

### Is Easter a public holiday in Georgia?

Yes: by law, the four days from Friday to Monday are non-working days; by our count, 30 April to 3 May in 2027.[^1]

### Are banks and shops open at Easter?

Not all. Government offices and many private businesses, including banks, do not work; restaurants and cafés usually do.[^4]

### Why is Orthodox Easter later than Western Easter?

The two dates are calculated differently, so the gap changes from year to year; in 2028 they coincide.[^2][^3][^11]

### What do Georgians do on Easter Monday?

Families go to the cemetery to pray for the dead.[^6] The law calls the day All Souls' Day.[^1]

### What do Georgians say at Easter?

"Christ is risen!" The answer: "Truly he is risen!"[^5]
