Easter in Georgia (the country): 2 May 2027, public holidays, traditions and staying safe

Easter in Georgia — the country of Georgia, not the US state — falls on 2 May in 2027 by the Orthodox calendar, five weeks after Western Easter. This guide covers the four-day break and what closes, red eggs and food, remembering the dead, crowds and wine at the table.

When is Easter in Georgia? Dates for 2026–2028

In 2027 the feast falls on Sunday 2 May, according to the Orthodox calendar. Orthodox Easter 2027 is five weeks after the Western date, 28 March.

The date moves but always falls in April or May. For April and May weather, see Spring and autumn: Georgia in May and October.

YearOrthodoxWesternHolidays (our count)

20272 May28 March30 April – 3 May
202816 April16 April14–17 April

Our count from the law; the government may add extra days by ordinance. 9 April 2026 was also a day off.

Why is Georgian Easter later than Western Easter?

The Orthodox calendar sets the Georgian date; the Western one is set differently. The gap: five weeks in 2027, none in 2028, one week in 2026.

Public holidays at Easter in Georgia: what closes

Four Easter days are public holidays in Georgia: Good Friday, Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday and Easter Monday, which Article 30 of the Labour Code calls All Souls' Day. The same article lists Christmas on 7 January and Mariamoba on 28 August.

Government offices and many private businesses do not work; restaurants and cafés stay open.

Our tip. Get lari in cash before the long weekend; see Cash or card in Georgia?.

Trains and the metro at Easter

We found no 2027 timetables. In 2026 the Tbilisi Metro was set to run free at Easter, from 23:00 to 04:00, and special buses to the cemeteries from 08:00 to 20:00. Extra trains were scheduled between the capital and Batumi and Poti for 8–14 April 2026. See How to buy Georgian Railway tickets and Tbilisi metro hours and intervals.

Georgian Easter traditions: red eggs, paska and the night in church

Easter is the main religious feast for Orthodox Christians. It follows the longest fast, 40 days.

On Palm Sunday believers sprout wheat on plates. Eggs are dyed red on Good Friday, traditionally with rose madder roots and onion skins. Most believers spend the night before the feast in church. People also hold egg-cracking contests.

Before a service, read Visiting churches; the UK government advises cultural sensitivity when photographing churches.

Chona, or Alilo, is a village custom: men sing from house to house, and the hostess lowers a basket of sweets, fruit and money from a balcony.

Easter Monday: a day for family graves

On Easter Monday people across the country visit their relatives' graves, pray for the dead and roll an egg over them.

What do people say at Easter?

People greet each other with "Christ is risen!" and answer "Truly he is risen!"

Easter food in Georgia: paska, lamb and the supra

The raisin cake is the centre of the festive table and symbolises Golgotha.

Lamb is eaten in the east, a suckling piglet in the capital and the west, with khachapuri, chakapuli, fish, greens and wine.

The Government of Canada advises: "Boil it, cook it, peel it, or leave it." See Food safety rules for travellers.

Staying safe over the Easter weekend

Dial 112 for an ambulance; outside Tbilisi, medical facilities are limited. Water quality varies; bottled water is widely sold.

Crime levels are low, but watch for pickpockets in crowded places. Amid ongoing protests, the UK government advises avoiding large gatherings and demonstrations; the Government of Canada notes they are frequent, especially in central Tbilisi.

See How to call an ambulance in Georgia: dial 112 and Is it safe to travel to Georgia right now? The protests. For a non-urgent problem, the catalogue of doctors and clinics in Tbilisi on 100doc.ge is one place to look.

Wine at the supra and driving

If you drive, do not drink: more than 0.3 ‰ of alcohol in a driver's blood counts as drunk driving. Up to 0.7 ‰ means a six-month licence suspension, more means a year.

The CDC advises limiting alcohol. The Government of Canada describes a "zero-tolerance policy" on drink-driving, and the UK government advises avoiding alcohol if driving. See Drink driving.

Our tip. Pick a sober driver before the meal.

Before Friday 30 April: get cash, buy train tickets early, save the emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

When is Easter in Georgia in 2027?

Sunday 2 May, according to the Orthodox calendar.

Is Easter a public holiday in Georgia?

Yes: by law, the four days from Friday to Monday are non-working days; by our count, 30 April to 3 May in 2027.

Are banks and shops open at Easter?

Not all. Government offices and many private businesses, including banks, do not work; restaurants and cafés usually do.

Why is Orthodox Easter later than Western Easter?

The two dates are calculated differently, so the gap changes from year to year; in 2028 they coincide.

What do Georgians do on Easter Monday?

Families go to the cemetery to pray for the dead. The law calls the day All Souls' Day.

What do Georgians say at Easter?

"Christ is risen!" The answer: "Truly he is risen!"
